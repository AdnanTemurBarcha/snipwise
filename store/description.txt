Snipwise Studio captures any web page — then lets you edit it like a real image editor. It's free, open source, and lives entirely in your browser. No accounts. No cloud uploads. Everything stays on your device.

5 WAYS TO START
- Visible Area — capture exactly what's on screen right now
- Full Page — scroll and stitch the entire page automatically
- Crop Region — drag to select any area of the page
- Save as PDF — one-click capture straight to a PDF file
- Blank Canvas — start from scratch: pick a preset (social, screen, print) or any custom size, with a transparent or colored background

A FULL LAYERED EDITOR — NOT JUST A MARKUP TOOL
Every capture opens in Studio, a complete editor:
- Layers — every image, annotation and brush stroke is its own layer: move, resize, rotate, flip, reorder, lock, hide, duplicate, rename
- Pages — keep several pages in one project, each with its own size and layers
- Align, distribute and snap with guide lines; multi-select with a marquee
- Add images by file picker, drag-and-drop or paste — they keep their real size, and transparent margins (like a window screenshot's shadow) are trimmed so the transform box hugs what you see
- Non-destructive crop, artboard resize, zoom and pan
- Undo and redo for up to 300 steps, each one labelled

ANNOTATE
- Text and Callout bubbles — real objects: double-click to edit, any font size, weight and color
- Rectangle, Ellipse, Line and Arrow — outline, fill, corner radius, arrowheads
- Pen and Highlighter — freehand strokes
- Step markers — auto-numbered circles for tutorials
- Emoji stamps — search the full Unicode emoji set, with recents and quick picks

HIDE PRIVATE DETAILS
- Blur, Pixelate or Solid cover any area. They are non-destructive layers: move or delete one and the original is back

PAINT AND RETOUCH
- Brush (size, hardness, opacity, pen pressure), Eraser, Paint bucket, Gradient, Eyedropper and Clone stamp
- Selections: rectangle, ellipse, lasso and magic wand — add, subtract, feather, grow or shrink, invert, then fill, clear, or copy to a new layer
- Paint lands on its own layer, so your screenshot stays untouched

EFFECTS, ADJUSTMENTS AND MASKS
- Drop shadow and outline on any layer
- Brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, sepia, grayscale, invert, blur and Levels — all non-destructive
- Adjustment layers that re-color everything beneath them, plus presets you can save yourself
- 16 blend modes and per-layer opacity
- Layer masks: hide parts of a layer without erasing them; paint masks with the brush or create one from a selection
- Filters: Gaussian blur, sharpen, noise, pixelate, posterize, black & white, sepia (limited to your selection if you have one)

EXPORT
- Download PNG — full resolution, with transparency preserved
- Copy Image — paste straight into Slack, email, Figma, anywhere
- Export PDF — a dialog with a live preview: page size (A4/Letter/Legal), orientation, margins, fit or split tall pages, and an optional cover page with your own title and accent color. Combines every page into one document

BUILT-IN DOCUMENTATION
Click Docs in the editor (or press F1) for a searchable guide to every tool and shortcut. Light and dark themes included.

PRIVACY
Snipwise Studio is 100% local. Screenshots and edits never leave your device. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services of any kind. It makes no network requests of its own, and it asks for no host permissions.

Permissions used:
- activeTab — to capture the page you invoked it on
- scripting — to show the crop overlay and run the full-page scroll capture
- tabs — to take the screenshot and open the editor, PDF and docs tabs
- storage — to keep your theme and saved adjustment presets (screenshots are never stored)

OPEN SOURCE
Snipwise Studio is MIT-licensed, written in TypeScript and built with Vite, with a unit and end-to-end test suite. Read the code, report issues or contribute on GitHub: https://github.com/AdnanTemurBarcha/snipwise
